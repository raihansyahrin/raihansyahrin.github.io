About

Hi, I'm Raihan.

I'm a mobile engineer based in Jakarta, currently at PT Budhi Futura Incresca. Most of my work is in Flutter, and I also build what sits around the app: React frontends, FastAPI services, and the AI models behind them.

I work day to day with UI/UX designers in Figma, backend engineers on API contracts, and project managers on scope and deadlines. I flag risks early, plan work into releases that land on time, and look after the app once it's live: tests, release pipelines, and crash monitoring.
